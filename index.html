§ 01 — Rounds & tasks

A cold-storage round in under a minute.

Open the round and the first unit is already focused. Type the reading, press Enter, and it saves with who observed it and when, then moves to the next fridge. Nothing expands, nothing reorders, the page never jumps.

  • Out-of-range values warn before you save, then open an exception automatically.
  • Late entries and “no testing today” both require a reason, and are never counted as observations.
  • Missed, due and overdue work in one list, with supervisors notified instead of finding out at audit.

Try it → type a temperature and press Enter. Try 9.2.

§ 02 — Reagent traceability & recalls

Recall a bottle of methanol and IONLOGS follows it through calibrators and mobile phases — even two steps away — to every plate, instrument and result ID. New use is blocked; a supervisor override is audited.

§ 03 — Columns & wear parts

Know a column is tired before your QC does.

Confirmed injections from each plate land in the column’s ledger with a running total. Warn and replace limits are set by your lab, and the alert appears on Home before the limit, not after a failed batch.

  • Lamps, pump seals and guard columns tracked by injections, hours or days installed.
  • Replacement reminders open as tasks within your lead time and close when the swap is recorded.
  • Replacing a column retires it with its full history kept.

§ 05 — Roles

One lab. Four ways in.

Everyone sees the same records through the lens of their job — and each lab sees only its own.

Te

Technician

Rounds, My Work, receiving lots, preparations and plates. Walk mode for a full-screen keypad on the move.

Su

Supervisor

Exceptions to acknowledge or close with a note, a keyboard review queue, recalls and overrides.

Ad

Admin

Lab settings, closures, versioned task definitions with a reason for every change, users and equipment.
